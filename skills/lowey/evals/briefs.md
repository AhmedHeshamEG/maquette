# Eval briefs

Fifteen briefs that cover what the skill must do well. `run_evals.py` reads the blocks below (`## id` + the brief, and
optional `voiceover:`, `framing:`, `seconds:` lines), runs each in a fresh scene, and scores the result with the
rubric (`rubric.md`).

## night-desk
A night desk scene in Berlin, 1941: an Enigma machine, a desk lamp, papers. Ink Look. The lamp is the only warm light;
the subject is the machine.
seconds: 5

## cave-question
A robot discovers a glowing question mark in a dark cave. Sketch Look with one accent: the robot. The question mark
pops in; the robot reacts.
seconds: 6

## kitchen-argument
Two Blob characters argue in a kitchen: one points, the other shrugs. Over-the-shoulder, then a two-shot.
seconds: 6

## computer-room
A room full of computers, all screens dark but one. The lit screen is the subject. Comic Look on twos.
seconds: 5

## space-window
A space station window with the Earth outside; a Blob astronaut looks out. Quiet, cool light, the Earth as the rim.
seconds: 6

## classroom-reveal
A classroom: a maths equation is revealed on the board on the word "equals". Medium shot, the board on a third.
voiceover: Two plus two equals four.
seconds: 4

## dusk-street
A street at dusk with shop signs lighting up one by one. Wide, then a push in on the last sign.
seconds: 6

## door-hesitation
A character walks to a door and hesitates before opening it. Full shot; the walk arcs; a hold before the hand moves.
seconds: 7

## comic-impact
A comic-look action beat on twos: something smashes into a wall with an impact burst on "BOOM".
voiceover: Then, BOOM.
seconds: 3

## sketch-quiet
A Sketch-look quiet moment: an old letter on a table by a window, one accent colour on the wax seal. Almost no motion.
seconds: 5

## vertical-short
A 9:16 vertical shot for Shorts: a Blob holds up a phone showing a notification, framed safely for the vertical UI.
framing: 9:16
seconds: 4

## three-shot-story
A three-shot sequence synced to a 12-second voiceover: the message (desk), nobody could (computer room), the secret
(robot in a cave). Cut on "People" and "Last".
voiceover: In 1941, a message was encrypted with Enigma. People tried for decades. Nobody could. Last, the secret was out.
seconds: 12

## web-screen
A newsroom at night: on the wall, a screen shows a bar chart (a web piece) whose bars grow on the word "doubled". Comic
Look. The screen is the subject; a Blob anchor stands beside it.
voiceover: In one year the number of readers doubled.
seconds: 5

## lottie-title
A title over the frame, made as a Lottie animation that writes itself in, over a calm wide shot of a desert road at
golden hour. The title says "Route 66". Painterly Look.
seconds: 5

## hand-off
Build a small product shot of a ceramic mug on a table, then get it ready for Hesham: the Look panel open, the mug
selected, the playhead at 2 s, and a USDZ of the scene exported for his client's iPhone.
seconds: 4
