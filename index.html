Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no account, no "Pro" version waiting later. The whole thing.

Why give it away?

It was going to be $14.99. I'd rather everyone with an iPad can make 3D than have it be another paid app. If you want to help, there's a Support button.

Android? Windows?

Maquette is native, made for iPad and Pencil, Mac next (that's the $700 checkpoint). It's open source, so anyone can port it. If you do, tell me, I want to see it.

Which iPads?

Any iPad on iPadOS 26. The Pencil is best. A finger draws too, just with worse handwriting.

I've never done 3D. Is that a problem?

That's the person it was made for.

Was it made with AI?

I designed it and made every decision; Claude helped me write the code. Nothing inside the app generates art. What's in your frame is what you put there.

Can I use what I make commercially?

Yes. It's yours. The Looks and shaders you export come with a licence that says "use them in anything".

Can I sell my own copy of the app?

No. The GPL keeps every copy free and open, and the name and the icon stay mine (TRADEMARKS.md).

Where does the money go?

Into Maquette: the App Store fee, a Mac to port it, then time. One post a month on Patreon shows exactly what it paid for.

Can I help with code?

Fixes, translations and ports, yes please. New features: open an issue first. How.
