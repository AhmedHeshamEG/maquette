# Test-week tasks

One task per feature for the two test weeks (between M14 and M16), written for its person in `studio-h/USERS.md`. A
task says what to make, never which buttons to press; the tester says where they got stuck.

## 0.19 (P1): physics

- **The curious teenager:** a wooden crate and a steel ball dropped from the top of the Keeper's Tower, landing at the
  same moment, then a glass on the table that breaks when the ball hits it.
- **The engineering student:** a door on a hinge that a motor swings open, a pendulum on a rope, and a stack of blocks
  that "does it stand?" says will tip, then fixed so it stands.
- **The architecture student:** the shadows of a small house in Rome across 21 June, from 7:00 to 19:00, played as one
  shot.
- **The sculptor:** a rigged creature that falls down the tower's stairs, kept as keys, with one arm fixed by hand.
- **Everyone:** water poured into a glass until it's half full, a flag that flutters in the wind, and smoke rising from
  the keeper's lamp; then the whole shot exported as a video.

## 0.18 (U7): texture

- **The texture artist:** a wooden crate beside the Keeper's Tower: planks, dirt in the corners, worn metal corners
  that shine, a stencilled label on one side, and the same scratch on both sides at once. Export it for a game engine
  with all its maps.
- **The game developer:** a low-poly lantern coloured face by face, out as a glTF with vertex colours and again with a
  palette texture; then a heavy sculpt's detail on its light copy.
- **The Procreate person:** a fox with soft fur, longer on its tail than its face, and a colour picked from the
  scene's palette.
- **Everyone:** drag Gold from the materials onto the keeper's lamp; make the lamp's glass glow.

## 0.17 (H1): editable history

- **The maker:** the 40 × 20 × 10 mm block with a 6 mm hole and rounded corners; then the screw is bigger than planned:
  make the hole 8 mm without building it again, and print it.

## 0.16 (U6): shape

- **The maker:** a box with a lid that fits it, 60 × 40 × 30 mm, built by pulling faces and typing sizes, the lid's
  edges rounded.
- **The sculptor:** take a rock from the Kit, make one side bulge out and smooth the other, both sides alike.
- **The game developer:** turn a lumpy sculpt into a clean model under 2 000 faces and check its triangle count.
- **The engineering student:** a sketch of a bracket whose holes stay the same size and level when its length changes.
- **Everyone:** a twisted tower beside the Keeper's Tower that untwists while the camera circles it, and a row of five
  windows where changing one changes them all.

## 0.15 (U5): draw again & the grammar

- **The Procreate person:** draw a tree beside the Keeper's Tower with lines in the air, then a vine climbing the
  tower's wall drawn straight onto it. Pick one branch and make it thicker.
- **The flipbook animator:** a bird flapping its wings in three drawings beside the tower, looping, without opening
  the timeline.
- **The maker:** a 40 × 20 mm plate with a round hole, drawn from shapes and pulled into a solid, sizes typed.
- **The explainer maker:** a title, "The Keeper", that stays on the picture while the camera circles the tower.
- **Everyone:** carry the keeper from the ground onto the tower's lowest step with one finger, then put a lamp on the
  table with "To the ground".
