# Test-week tasks

One task per feature for the two test weeks (between M14 and M16), written for its person in `studio-h/USERS.md`. A
task says what to make, never which buttons to press; the tester says where they got stuck.

## 0.15 (U5): draw again & the grammar

- **The Procreate person:** draw a tree beside the Keeper's Tower with lines in the air, then a vine climbing the
  tower's wall drawn straight onto it. Pick one branch and make it thicker.
- **The flipbook animator:** a bird flapping its wings in three drawings beside the tower, looping, without opening
  the timeline.
- **The maker:** a 40 × 20 mm plate with a round hole, drawn from shapes and pulled into a solid, sizes typed.
- **The explainer maker:** a title, "The Keeper", that stays on the picture while the camera circles the tower.
- **Everyone:** carry the keeper from the ground onto the tower's lowest step with one finger, then put a lamp on the
  table with "To the ground".
