# Lesson lines

One line per feature for the person it serves (`studio-h/USERS.md`), in Hesham's voice, for the YouTube lessons the
Learn place links to (CONTEXT §8). A phase adds the lines of what it built; M16 records them.

## 0.25 (G1): guest kits

- **The architect:** "Scene, Set up for Room. Model, Add, Building: tap the corners and the walls follow; tap the first
  corner to close the room. Roof: tap the corners on top of the walls. Plans: every room with its area, ready to print."
- **The architect:** "Underlay: drop the professor's PDF. Measure a wall you know, type its length: the plan is to scale.
  Trace it with Walls. Export, 3D, IFC: it opens in Revit as walls, floors and a roof."
- **The architect:** "Match a photo: two red lines along one wall, two blue along the other, the yellow dot on the floor.
  Match: your building stands in the photo."
- **The maker:** "Set up for Print. Model, Add, Jar and lid: the lid screws on, its thread a fit looser. Export, 3D, 3MF:
  each colour gets its own filament."
- **The maker:** "Model, Edit, 3D print: Screw hole, M3, insert, then tap where it goes. Split: two parts with pegs. Show
  overhangs: the red needs support; Best way up turns it so it doesn't."
- **The engineering student:** "Set up for Study. Group your parts, Model, Edit, Explode: they fly apart. Export, Paper,
  Technical drawing: three views, the sizes and a parts list with each part's mass."
- **The game developer:** "Set up for Game. Model, Add, Greybox: floors, walls, ramps on the metre grid. Export, 3D, Game
  level, For Godot: every piece is solid when it lands in your project."
- **The illustrator:** "Characters, New, Mannequin. Pose it, light it, Export, Picture, Layered PSD: open it in Procreate
  and draw over every layer."
- **Everyone:** "Export, Paper, Papercraft: your model unfolded flat, every tab numbered to the edge it glues to."

## 0.24 (L2): Looks & the library

- **The anime animator:** "Look, Style, Anime cel: two hard tones and a lavender shadow, on twos. Select the hero, Look,
  Finish, Focus lines: they close in on him and follow him as he runs."
- **The Procreate person:** "Drop your Procreate file on the stage: every layer stands up as a sheet, the sky at the back.
  Push the camera in slowly and the layers part."
- **The illustrator:** "Painterly: the strokes follow the shapes. Hold it, Duplicate, and in the Look Studio make the
  strokes bigger; Save to Yours and it's in every project."
- **The explainer maker:** "Library, Things, Grounds, Snow: the whole floor is snow, lit by the Look like everything on it."
- **The comic maker:** "Look, Finish, Print: Ben-Day dots on any Look, lines that boil, and the out-of-focus things as
  slipped plates. Newsprint under it all."
- **Everyone:** "Library, Yours, Share a pack: pick your Looks and brushes, write your name, say what others may do.
  AirDrop it; on their iPad it opens with your name on every thing."

## 0.21 (A1): animate & export

- **The explainer maker:** "Pick the car, Animate, Path, and draw where it goes. Draw slow, it drives slow; speed up, it
  speeds up. It turns into the corners by itself."
- **The explainer maker:** "Look at: tap the ball, and her head follows it. Pick up: tap her hand at this moment, and the
  cup goes with her."
- **The flipbook animator:** "Drag the ruler: the ghosts show where it was and where it's going. Dope sheet: every key,
  property by property."
- **The indie game developer:** "Export, 3D, glTF, For Godot. Then The Look as a shader, For Godot: your game looks like
  your Maquette scene."
- **The indie filmmaker:** "Export, Video, Render passes: colour, alpha, depth and a mask for every thing, for your
  compositor. Switch on Final for motion blur."
- **Everyone:** "Properties: tap Glow and it's keyed. Change it later in the timeline, and it animates."

## 0.20 (K1): characters

- **The indie game developer:** "Library, Characters, New, Nothing. Tap Eyes: one big eye. Tap Mouth. It's rigged
  already. For games, Godot: it lands in your project with its clips and its face."
- **The explainer maker:** "New, Person. Face: tap Oh, Ee, Surprised to see them. Then Say a line, or lip sync your
  voiceover: fourteen mouths, chosen for you."
- **The Procreate person:** "Draw a mouth on the face with the Pencil, tap it, Make it a part, as the Oh. Your own
  drawing, in its place, every time it says Oh."
- **Everyone:** "Proportions: drag the head up to make it bigger, a foot down for longer legs. The feet stay on the
  ground."
- **Everyone:** "Act it out: film yourself, and the character copies you."

## 0.19 (P1): physics

- **The curious teenager:** "Pick anything, open Real, choose what it's made of. Press play: it falls with the weight of
  a real one. Wood and steel land together, Galileo was right."
- **The curious teenager:** "Make a glass real and drop it. Glass breaks. Pour water into another one: Model, Add, Pour
  water, above the glass."
- **The engineering student:** "Select two things, Join them: a hinge, a slider, a spring, a rope. Give the hinge a motor
  and it turns. Show the forces to see the arrows."
- **The engineering student:** "Does it stand? Your tower's centre of mass, a plumb line, and the footprint it stands on.
  Green stands, red tips."
- **The architecture student:** "Look, World, Real sun: your city, your date. Switch on the shadow study and the
  timeline runs the day: play it for the professor."
- **The sculptor:** "Make your rigged creature real and it falls like a ragdoll. Keep it, and the fall is keys you can
  fix by hand."
- **Everyone:** "Behind the scene's name: gravity (try the Moon), wind, when things start to move. Hold a thing for
  Settle: it lies down the way it would."

## 0.18 (U7): texture

- **The texture artist:** "Layers paint more than colour: tap Roughness and paint where it's matte, Metal where it
  shines. Swipe a layer for a mask."
- **The texture artist:** "Drag a material onto anything. Hold one and Paint it on: the brush paints where it shows."
- **The texture artist:** "Surface, Dirt: grime settles in the creases by itself. Worn edges: the paint rubs off the
  corners. The amount is one slider."
- **The texture artist:** "Mirror on, paint one cheek, get both. Seams: tap the edges to cut, then Cut, and paint it
  flat."
- **The game developer:** "The faces brush fills a whole face with one tap. Export glTF, Painted colour: Vertex colours
  or Palette."
- **The sculptor:** "Bake normals from your sculpt onto its light copy: the detail stays, the weight goes."
- **Everyone:** "Surface, Fur: soft fur or hair cards. Paint the Fur map to keep it short where you want."
- **The explainer maker:** "Look, Hatching: shadows drawn like a pen sketch."

## 0.16 (U6): shape

- **The maker:** "Pick the shape, then touch a face with the Pencil and drag it. An edge rounds, a corner moves. No
  buttons to choose first."
- **The maker:** "Tap a side's number in the panel and type 40: the shape keeps its sizes."
- **The sculptor:** "Model, Brush: grab pulls the surface, smooth evens it, inflate puffs it up. Mirror is on, so both
  cheeks change together."
- **The game developer:** "Shape of this object, Clean up the shape: a messy sculpt becomes even quads a game engine
  likes. Watch the triangle count."
- **The engineering student:** "Tap two lines of a sketch and choose Square or Parallel; lock a size and change another,
  and the drawing keeps its rules."
- **Everyone:** "Bend, twist, taper, bulge: one handle. Drag it, and key it like any move to make it wobble."
- **Everyone:** "The cube in the corner: tap Front to look from the front, double-tap for a flat drawing view."

## 0.15 (U5): draw again & the grammar

- **The Procreate person:** "Tap Draw and draw. Over a thing, the line lies on it; in the air, it lands on the sheet in
  front of you. No setup, ever."
- **The Procreate person:** "Brush, Smudge, Erase, Sheets, Colour: the five you know. Tap the brush again for the
  library; Solids and Shapes are sets in it."
- **The flipbook animator:** "Sheets, Draw frame by frame. Draw, flick right with a finger for the next frame, left to
  go back. Tap the time for onion skins."
- **The maker:** "Shapes: drag a rectangle corner to corner, tap its numbers to type them, then pull its arrow up into a
  solid."
- **Everyone:** "Every number is a slider: drag it slowly for fine, fast for big moves, or tap it to type."
- **Everyone:** "Two fingers always move the view. One finger carries what you picked over whatever's under it."
- **Everyone:** "Squeeze the Pencil: the lasso, the eyedropper, the eraser, snapping, hide the sheet, frame. Hold a
  finger on a thing while drawing to take its colour."
- **The explainer maker:** "Titles are 3D text: switch on Stick to the camera and it stays on the picture while the
  camera flies."

## 0.23 (D1): motion design

- **The motion designer:** "Model, Add, Logo: pick the client's SVG. Each colour stands up as a solid; push the front
  face to make it deeper."
- **The motion designer:** "Select your text, Font, Bring in a font: the brand's own. Then Letters, Fly in: they arrive one
  after another from the playhead."
- **The motion designer:** "Hold the logo, More, Clone: twenty-five copies. Add an effect, Push, Where: Ripple. Then More
  ways to move, Move to the music, Beat: the ripple comes with every kick."
- **The explainer maker:** "Two gears: select one, Link to another thing, Turns against it, tap the other. Spin the first
  and both turn."
- **The hobby sculptor:** "Hold the cube, More, Morph into, tap the ball: it becomes the ball over a second. Drag its keys to
  say when."
- **Everyone:** "Export says whether it loops seamlessly. If it jumps, Close the loop: spins and ripples are fitted to the
  loop's length."
